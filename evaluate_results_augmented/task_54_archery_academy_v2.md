### Evaluation Result
TASK 54_archery_academy_v2 | Solvability: YES | Plausibility: Good | Diversity: Good

### Justification
- Solvability: The task requires 4 wooden bows (4 logs + 4 string) and 20 arrows (20 sticks from ~2 logs + 20 feathers). With 2 lumberjacks (level 20 lumberjacking) harvesting 8+ logs in 55 steps, and 2 fletchers (level 20 fletching, above the level 5 requirement for bows and level 1 for arrows) with the required 4 string and 20 feathers already distributed between them, this is achievable within the action budget.

- Plausibility: The task genuinely requires collaboration as lumberjacks have the axes and lumberjacking skill to gather logs, while fletchers have the fletching skill and starting materials (string, feathers) needed for crafting. Material transfers between agents are necessary since lumberjacks cannot efficiently craft and fletchers cannot efficiently harvest.

- Diversity: The task combines resource gathering (lumberjacking for logs), material processing (crafting sticks from logs), and equipment crafting (bows and arrows). It involves a multi-step production chain with different agent roles and requires coordination for resource distribution between gatherers and crafters.
