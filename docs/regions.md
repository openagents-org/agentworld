## Navigation Tips

- Regions are organized in a grid system based on 100x100 tile sections
- Coordinate format: Region X-Y corresponds to tiles (X*100) to ((X+1)*100), (Y*100) to ((Y+1)*100)
- Minibosses typically offer better rewards and achievements
- Some creatures are roaming and may move between nearby coordinates
- Higher level regions often contain valuable resources and rare drops
