- **Mob Key**: Internal identifier used by the game
- **Mob Name**: Display name shown to players  
- **Level**: Mob's difficulty level
- **Region**: Spawn coordinates or area (if specified in spawns.json)
- **Item Drops**: Items dropped with rates (per 10,000 chance unless noted)
- **Special Notes**: Boss status, special abilities, etc.

## Drop Rate Notes

- Drop rates are calculated per 10,000 (0.01% = 1, 1% = 100, 10% = 1000)
- Higher level mobs generally have better drop rates and more valuable items
- Boss mobs have guaranteed drops of rare items
- Special spawn locations often have enhanced drop tables
- Some mobs use additional drop tables defined in `tables.json`

## Regions and Spawning

- Most mobs spawn in multiple locations across the world map
- Specific coordinates in spawns.json indicate unique instances with special properties
- Region information is determined by map coordinates and area definitions
- Boss mobs are typically in dedicated boss chambers or special areas
- Some mobs are restricted to specific biomes (ice, desert, aquatic, etc.)
